
# 📊 Superstore: What Sells, Where It Pays?

Welcome to my **Superstore Sales Data Analysis Project!** 👋

This project was developed as part of the **RevoU Data Analytics Bootcamp**, using Tableau to explore sales performance, product trends, and regional profitability across the United States from 2020 to 2023.

The main objective is to transform sales transaction data into meaningful business insights through data visualization and exploratory analysis.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding three key business questions:

- 📈 **What sells?** Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- 🌎 **Where does it pay?** Explore profit distribution across different states.
- 💰 **How is the business performing?** Analyze sales trends, revenue, and profitability over time.

---

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The analysis uses the **Sample Superstore Dataset**, provided as part of the RevoU project materials.

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|

### Key Variables

- **Order Date:** Date when an order was placed.
- **Category:** Main product category.
- **Sub-Category:** Product classification within each category.
- **Sales:** Revenue generated from product sales.
- **Profit:** Profit generated from transactions.
- **State:** Geographic location of the transaction.

## 📊 Analysis & Visualizations

### 1️⃣ Overall Sales Performance

Analyzed the overall business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs).

Key metrics:
- **Total Sales:** $2.33M
- **Total Profit:** $292K
- **Total Orders:** 5,111
- **Sales Growth (2023):** 21.4%

### 2️⃣ Sales Trend by Category

Examined monthly sales trends across three main product categories:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

The analysis helps identify sales fluctuations, seasonal patterns, and category performance over time.

### 3️⃣ Geographic Profitability Analysis

Visualized profit distribution across US states using a geographical map to identify high-performing and loss-making regions.

Key findings:
- California generated the highest profit ($76.4K).
- New York followed with approximately $74.0K in profit.
- Texas recorded the largest loss, approximately -$25.7K.

### 4️⃣ Top 5 Sub-Categories

Identified the five sub-categories with the highest total sales:

| Rank | Sub-Category | Total Sales |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Chairs | $335.8K |
| 2 | Phones | $331.8K |
| 3 | Storage | $224.6K |
| 4 | Tables | $208.0K |
| 5 | Binders | $207.4K |

### 5️⃣ Sales Performance Across All Sub-Categories

Compared sales performance across all product sub-categories to understand their respective contributions to overall revenue.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the analysis, several important findings were identified:

- **Technology Leads:** Technology generates the highest sales among the three product categories, contributing approximately $839.9K.
- **Top-Selling Products:** Chairs and Phones are the leading sub-categories in terms of total sales.
- **Regional Profitability:** California and New York generate the highest profits, while Texas records the largest loss.
- **Furniture Profitability:** Despite generating approximately $754.7K in sales, Furniture has a relatively low profit margin of around 2.6%, indicating potential profitability issues.

### 📌 Business Recommendations

- Strengthen strategies for high-performing product categories.
- Review pricing, discount, and cost strategies for less profitable products.
- Investigate loss-making regions to identify potential operational or pricing issues.
- Monitor sales trends to support better inventory and sales planning.
